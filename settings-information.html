About Hollow Night Camp

A place where stories unfold after dark.

Hollow Night Camp is a web-based storytelling experience built for readers who enjoy immersive narratives, hidden mysteries, and a distinctive atmosphere. Beyond reading, the platform aims to create a space where readers can explore, participate, and become part of a growing community.

Terms & Community Guidelines

Help keep the camp welcoming for everyone.

By using Hollow Night Camp, users are expected to respect other readers, follow applicable platform rules, and use community features responsibly.

Our community guidelines encourage:

  • Respectful and constructive discussions.
  • No harassment, hate speech, or targeted abuse.
  • No spam, scams, or malicious links.
  • Respect for creators and intellectual property.
  • Responsible use of platform features.
  • Appropriate reporting of harmful content or technical issues.
