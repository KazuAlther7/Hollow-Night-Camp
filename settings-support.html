Getting Started

Sign in with your camp account, then use the Main Bar to move around: Campfire for the latest chapter, Novels to browse stories, Theories for community discussion, and Arcade for games.

Reading & Chapters

Open a novel and pick a chapter to start reading. Your progress and bookmarks are kept under Settings › History & Bookmarks, so you can resume where you left off.

Account & Profile

Change your nickname, bio and profile picture under Settings › Profile.

Display Settings

Choose the theme (System, Light or Dark) and the language (English or Indonesian) under Settings › Display. Both are saved on your device.
